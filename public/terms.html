Using Lapped

Lapped is a free personal utility for adding a High Park lap receipt to your own qualifying Strava activities. You may use it only with a Strava account you are authorized to connect.

Your activities

You remain responsible for your Strava activities, their visibility, and their descriptions. Lapped only writes its receipt after detecting a completed target segment effort. It is not a training, safety, navigation, or emergency service.

Strava

Lapped is an independent project that is compatible with Strava and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Strava. Your use of Strava remains subject to Strava’s own terms and policies.

Availability and changes

Lapped is provided as available. Features may change or be paused to protect reliability, security, or compliance. You can disconnect at any time from Lapped or through your Strava settings.

Questions and requests

For support, privacy, correction, or deletion requests, use the request form on the Lapped home page and begin privacy requests with “privacy:”.
